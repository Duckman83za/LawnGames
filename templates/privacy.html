Privacy notice

Shaun's Events, trading as Lawn Game Rentals, respects your privacy. This notice explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, and what your rights are under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

Enquiry form. When you send an enquiry we receive the name, email address, event details and message you provide. The form is processed by Formspree, which delivers it to us by email; Formspree's own privacy policy applies to that transmission.

WhatsApp, phone and email. If you contact us directly, we hold the details you share for the purpose of responding and, if you book, arranging your hire.

Bookings. To release equipment we require a copy of the responsible person's ID and a signed indemnity form. These are kept for the duration of the hire and for our records afterwards, as required to manage deposits and any claims.

Website analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used - pages viewed, approximate location, device type. This relies on cookies and is aggregated; it does not identify you personally to us. You can block analytics cookies in your browser without affecting the site.

Why we use it

To respond to your enquiry, to arrange and manage a hire, to refund your deposit, to meet our legal and insurance obligations, and to improve the website. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted within 12 months. Booking records, including ID copies and indemnity forms, are kept for as long as necessary to manage the hire and any deposit or damage claim, and then for the period required by South African tax and record-keeping law.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email shaun@lawngamerentals.co.za and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.
